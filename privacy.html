Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Video Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the Ripple Effect with Milo YouTube channel.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, Video Studio uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API, only on the owner's own channel, to: upload the owner's own videos and thumbnails with their titles, descriptions, translated titles and descriptions, schedule and privacy setting, and the made-for-kids and altered-or-synthetic-content declarations; add those videos to the owner's own playlists; post one comment on the owner's own new videos; and read the owner's own channel statistics (views, watch time, estimated revenue and similar figures). It does not access anyone else's account, and it never comments on, edits or changes anyone else's videos or channels.

Where data is kept

Everything Video Studio reads is stored on the owner's own computer. To analyse it and write the owner's videos, Video Studio sends parts of it (the owner's own channel statistics, and the titles, view counts and comments of public videos) to Anthropic's Claude AI service, through the owner's own Anthropic account. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, shown to anyone else, or used for advertising.

To plan videos, Video Studio also reads public information about other videos through the YouTube Data API (titles, view counts and public comments). That information is used only to understand what viewers are interested in, is never shown to anyone else, and is deleted automatically within 30 days.

Google user data

Video Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The owner can delete all stored data by deleting the tool's data folder.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Ripple Effect with Milo YouTube channel.
